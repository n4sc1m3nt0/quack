## Round 25 — the shapes of the holds over the passes, M5 step 6 (v0.0.25)

- Decisions of round 24 confirmed by the owner (2026-11-10): `aggregate_shapes` as a pure function over `HoldShape` vectors, first the 2-pole, the shelf and the Measured table; a 4-pole hold gets no shape for now (dropped); D61 stays as it is.
- `wah_guided.h` / `wah_guided.cpp`: `ShapeSummary` (`model`, `center_freq`, `q`, `peak_gain_db`, `shelf_freq_hz`, `shelf_gain_db`, `table_freq_hz`, `table_db`, `kept`, `dropped`, `valid`) and `wah::aggregate_shapes(holds)`. A hold is usable if it is valid, its shape is valid and is a 2-pole, a 2-pole with shelf or a Measured table of `kShapeTablePoints` cells (both vectors), and all its numbers are finite (centre, Q, shelf corner and table frequencies above 0). The model is the one that more than half of the usable holds have; with no such majority nothing is valid and every hold is listed as dropped. The result is the median of each parameter over the kept holds (the mean of the two middle values for an even number) or of each cell of the table, over the tables on the grid of the first kept one (within 0.1 %). No error is given (aggregate_holds does that for the band-pass); a single usable hold is valid with kept 1.
- `test_guided.cpp`: 13 new checks (groups 31 and 32; 55 in total): medians (odd, even), the majority (3 to 1, 2 to 2, 1 to 1, three models), the model of the winner with its shelf corner and gain, every kind of unusable hold (4-pole, refused, NaN, None, Q 0, infinite gain, negative centre, invalid shape, NaN shelf corner or gain, corner 0, table of the wrong size, 63 frequencies, a NaN cell), tables (median of each cell, a grid 20 % off dropped), a single hold, no holds, and the holds of a pass from audio (heel 400 Hz, toe 1600 Hz, Q 5).
- Written first against a stub (9 of 11 failed at the time, before 31k and 31l; the 2 refusals pass with a stub). 21 mutations in throwaway copies, then 7 more after the first survivors: the survivors were the finiteness of the shelf corner and gain, the size of the table's frequency vector and the list of dropped holds when there is no majority (one of them was a mutant that crashed, which counts as killed); a test for each was added (31k, 31l) and all are now red or crash. The throwaway script only printed failed checks: a crash showed as a blank line, noticed and checked by hand.
- Round size: about 160 new lines in `wah_guided.h`, `wah_guided.cpp`, `test_guided.cpp`.
- ROADMAP: no new decision.
- Not done / not run: anything on real hardware; the 4-pole across passes (two centres, two Q); `capture_summary` does not call `aggregate_shapes` yet (the shapes of the holds are not in the summary); the profile (the format v1.1 is M6); the Capturer and `analyze()` are still there. Not covered: the median of a Measured table when the tables come from different pedals' positions (the caller's concern).

## Round 15 — the holds of all passes, M4 step 4 (v0.0.15)

- `wah_measure.h` / `wah_measure.cpp`: `aggregate_holds(heel_holds, toe_holds)` returns a `HoldsAggregate` with a `HoldSummary` per end: centre, Q and gain as the median weighted by the windows each hold was measured from (D47, to confirm), the standard error of each (relative for centre and Q, in dB for the gain, infinite below 3 kept holds: D45, formula of D35), how many holds were kept and the indices of those dropped. A hold is dropped when its centre is more than 15 %, its Q more than 25 % or its gain more than 2 dB from the median of all usable holds (the first two compared in log, as in D33); with fewer than 3 usable holds nothing is dropped for disagreement (D44).
