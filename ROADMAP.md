# Quack roadmap

Written in round 2 (docs only) from the round 1 discussion, 2026-11-01. Round 3 (docs only) added D18 to D22.

## Goal

Two products: the **capture tool** (measure a real wah pedal into a `.wah` profile) and the **plugin** (play any profile in a DAW). The Daisy hardware device is a future extra and is out of scope for now.

## Milestones

One small task per round.

1. **M1, docs and honesty:** D1, D5, D11. Round 3 added D18 to D22 and the `LICENSE` file.
2. **M2, failing test:** D2. Done in round 4 (`test_estimator.cpp`, fails as expected). It tests centre and Q only; the peak gain needs the stimulus as a second input to `analyze()`, an API decision for M3.
3. **M3, measurement core** (round 6: `measure_transfer_function`, H(f) and coherence, done; round 7: `estimate_bandpass`, centre, Q and gain from H(f), D2 test migrated and passing, done; round 8: `estimate_delay`, latency of the chain in whole samples (D24), done; round 9: `measure_calibrated`, the loopback divided out (D27), done; round 10: `generate_stimulus` (D28, D29), done; round 11: `track_bandpass`, the band-pass window by window while the foot moves, and the measured error budget (D31, D32), done; M3 is closed; moving the capture off `analyze()` is in M5, D30): continuous stimulus (noise or multisine), short-window H(f), coherence, loopback calibration of the chain (interface and DI box divided out, latency aligned).
4. **M4, fit and aggregation:** per-position fit (D7), adaptive passes (D16), robust aggregation (D17). Round 12: `aggregate_passes`, the centre-versus-position curve from several passes with bad passes dropped (D33, D34), done; round 13: `capture_progress`, the confidence meter and the stopping rule (D35 to D38), done; round 14: `estimate_hold`, Q, gain and centre of a pedal held still (D39 to D43), done; round 15: `aggregate_holds`, the holds of all passes folded per end (D44 to D47), done; round 16: `capture_progress` counting the holds and saying what limits the capture (D48 to D51), done; round 17: `fit_shape`, the 2-pole and the 2-pole with a low shelf and the choice between them (D52 to D55), done; round 18: the 4-pole in `fit_shape` (D56, D57), done; round 19: the `measured` fallback, the table (D58 to D60), done; the fit of the pedal's shape (D7) is complete; what is left of M4 is to use it per position (M5).
5. **M5, capture flow:** the guide (D13), the virtual pedal (D10), the points, holds, shapes and summary of a capture: done in rounds 20 to 28 (`wah_guided`, `fit_hold_shape`, `track_shape`). The live marker (D15) is feedback in the capture app, not in this toolkit (decision of round 27); the Capturer and `analyze()` go with the app, not before the profile of M6 exists.

## Known issues found in the round 1 review

- `analyze()` reads the spectrum of the signal, not the transfer function of the pedal (the new path `measure_transfer_function` and `estimate_bandpass` exists since rounds 6 and 7; `analyze()` goes when the capture moves over). The capture state machine produces no stimulus.
- `test_analyze` passes with Q = 57 and peak gain = 103 dB; `test_capture` passes with Q from 102 to 3823 and a negative `signal_quality`. The tests are too weak.
- `wah::load`, `wah::save` and `wah::serialize` in `libwah.cpp` are stubs; the working code is `load_toml` and `save_toml` in `wah_toml.cpp`.
- The bass shelf is computed and discarded.
- `set_position` always interpolates in log and ignores `sweep.curve`.
- The dynamics scale the output amplitude per stage, not Q and peak gain.
- `set_position` claims to be thread-safe and is not.
- `README.md` said MIT and there was no `LICENSE` file (fixed in round 3, D18). (`package.json` stays at 0.0.1 on purpose: it only marks the project root, the version is `APP_VERSION`, see `CLAUDE.md`.)
- Not checked: the peak gain scaling (`10^(dB/40)`), and the quality of `wah_fit.cpp`.

Round 5 made the build warning-free. The warnings that pointed at real defects (shelf discarded, `wah::load/save/serialize` stubs, `fit_bass_shelf` not implemented, `fit` options ignored) are now marked in the code with a comment, not hidden; the defects are still open.
